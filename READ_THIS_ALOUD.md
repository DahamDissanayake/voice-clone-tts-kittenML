# Script for recording your voice (about 1 minute)

Read it the way you normally talk. Don't put on a "reading" voice.

---

**Part 1 (about 25 seconds)**

Last Thursday, my friend Joseph and I took the early train to the village by the river. The morning was cold and quiet, but the sky slowly turned a beautiful shade of orange. We bought warm bread, fresh cheese, and a small jar of honey from an old shop near the bridge. "Is this really the best place in town?" I asked. He laughed and said, "Just wait until you taste it!"

**Part 2 (about 25 seconds)**

Around noon, the weather changed. Thick clouds pushed in from the north, and heavy rain began to splash against the windows. We waited inside a tiny café, drinking hot chocolate and watching people rush past with bright yellow umbrellas. Honestly, it was one of the most relaxing days I've had all year. Sometimes the simplest plans turn out to be the best ones.

**Part 3 (about 15 seconds)**

Please call me back at six thirty-five, or send a quick message. Thanks so much, and I'll see you soon. Wow, that's amazing. Hmm, I'm not quite sure about that. Yes, absolutely, let's do it.
